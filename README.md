# ✦ VERTEX: Astrophysical Universe & Celestial Atlas

An interactive, editorial space atlas detailing the gravitational mechanics of our solar system, internal planetary structures, stellar nucleosynthesis, discovered galaxies, supermassive black holes, alien exoplanet systems, and a cinematic hyperdrive warp flight simulator.

Styled in classical **Times New Roman** serif typography with celestial gold, obsidian spacetime, and interactive constellation vertex nodes.

## 🌟 Key Features

### 1. ✦ Interactive Spacetime Vertex Mesh
- **Cosmic Web Nodes**: Animated background canvas with floating celestial vertices that connect with delicate constellation threads and interact with the cursor.

### 2. 🪐 Real-Time Orbital Orrery & Vertex Triangulation
- **Live Orbit Simulation**: The Sun and all planets (Mercury through Pluto) revolve at scientifically proportional relative speeds.
- **Fixed Dropdown Contrast**: Custom dark theme ensures dropdown menus and options render cleanly on Windows and all browsers.
- **Vertex Triangulation Lines**: Golden geometric constellation vectors connecting planetary orbital nodes.
- **Asteroid Belt**: Over 350 animated particles circulating between Mars and Jupiter.
- **Controls**:
  - Play / Pause orbits
  - Warp rate multiplier slider (0.1x to 5.0x)
  - Interactive Pan (click & drag) & Zoom (mouse wheel or buttons)
  - "Lock Coordinate" dropdown to focus on any planet
  - Click any celestial body to open its deep-dive profile!

### 3. 🧬 Deep-Dive Celestial Profiles ("What it is, How it was built & Properties")
For every planet (plus The Central Star, Earth's Moon, and Pluto):
- **Overview & Classification**: Terrestrial / Gas Giant / Ice Giant / Star / Dwarf.
- **How It Was Built (Planetary Genesis)**: Accretion story, frost line condensation (2.7 AU), early collision history, and differentiation.
- **Internal Composition & Layered Slices**: Interactive cross-section diagrams showing Core, Mantle, Crust, and Atmosphere with depths, temperatures, and pressures.
- **Detailed Physical Properties**: Total mass, diameter, surface gravity (\(m/s^2\) & relative to Earth), distance from Sun (AU & km), rotation (day length), orbital period (year length), orbital speed, and surface temperatures (°C & °F).
- **Atmosphere & Climate Dynamics**: Chemical composition breakdown progress bars ($H_2, He, N_2, O_2, CO_2, CH_4$, etc.), atmospheric surface pressure, and extreme weather events.
- **Moons & Space Exploration**: Major moons (Europa, Titan, Io, Ganymede, Triton, etc.) and historic robotic missions (Apollo, Voyager 1 & 2, Cassini, New Horizons, Perseverance).

### 4. 🛸 Intergalactic Starmap & Hyperdrive Warp Flight Simulator
- **Discovered Galaxies Catalog**: Milky Way, Andromeda (M31), Triangulum (M33), Whirlpool (M51), Sombrero (M104), Messier 87 (Virgo A), and JADES-GS-z14-0 (the farthest galaxy ever discovered by James Webb, 13.5+ billion light-years away!).
- **Discovered Black Holes**: Sagittarius A* (Milky Way core), M87* (first directly imaged black hole), TON 618 (66-billion-solar-mass quasar), Gaia BH1 (closest to Earth, 1,560 ly), Cygnus X-1.
- **Alien Solar Systems (Exoplanets)**: TRAPPIST-1 (7 Earth-sized worlds, 3 in habitable zone), Proxima Centauri (closest star, Proxima b), Kepler-452 ("Earth's older cousin"), 55 Cancri (diamond planet), HD 189733 (glass-rain planet).
- **Cosmic Web Superstructures**: Laniakea Supercluster, Virgo Supercluster, Boötes Void, Hercules-Corona Borealis Great Wall.
- **Intergalactic Travel Calculator**: Select origin and destination to see exact **Light-Years**, trillions of kilometers, and travel time under:
  - Voyager 1 Speed ($61,200\text{ km/h}$)
  - Parker Solar Probe ($692,000\text{ km/h}$)
  - Relativistic Speed ($0.99 c$)
  - Alcubierre Warp 9.9 ($3,053\times c$)
- **Cinematic Hyperdrive Jump Sequence**:
  - Click **"ENGAGE HYPERDRIVE WARP JUMP"** to launch the cockpit HUD.
  - Relativistic star-stretching and glowing hyperspace tunnel animation.
  - Accelerating warp charge, sub-space pulse, and sonic boom audio via the Web Audio API.
  - Live light-year odometer countup and arrival clearance dossier!

### 5. 🌌 Universe & Deep Space Vertices
- **⏳ 13.8 Billion Year Cosmic Timeline**: Step-by-step chronology from the Big Bang Singularity, Cosmic Inflation, Recombination (CMB), Dark Ages, to peak galaxy formation and future cosmic heat death.
- **✨ Star Life Cycles**: Stellar nurseries, protostars, main sequence stars, red giants, planetary nebulae, white dwarfs, supernovae, neutron stars, and black holes.
- **🕳️ Black Holes & Curvature**: Interactive schematic of Event Horizon, Ergosphere, Accretion Disk, Relativistic Jets, and Hawking Radiation.
- **🔮 The 95% Dark Universe**: Donut chart and breakdown of Dark Energy (68.3%), Dark Matter (26.8%), and Ordinary Baryonic Matter (4.9%).
- **📐 Orders of Magnitude Cosmic Scale**: Interactive slider spanning from the Planck Length (\(10^{-35}\) m) to the Observable Universe (\(10^{27}\) m).

### 6. 🧪 Interactive Astro-Laboratory
- **⚖️ Side-by-Side Planet Comparator**: Compare any two celestial worlds side-by-side with relative scale preview and comparative metric bars.
- **🧮 Spacetime Weight & Age Calculator**: Enter your Earth weight and age to calculate your weight on the Sun, Moon, and all planets along with your chronological age in planetary years.
- **🏆 Astrophysical Knowledge Challenge**: 10 randomized interactive questions with instant feedback, explanations, score tracking, and astronomer rank certificate.

### 7. 🔊 Pure Synthesized Web Audio
- Built-in ethereal ambient space hum, warp charge, hyperspace whoosh, and UI sound cues generated live via the Web Audio API without requiring any external audio files.
